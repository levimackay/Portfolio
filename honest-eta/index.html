Honest ETA

An iPhone app that fixes "be there in 10" before you send it.

How it works

1. Pick where you're going.

2. The app takes the Apple Maps travel time and adds however late you usually run.

3. Tap Send. Your share sheet opens with one sentence ready: "I'll be there at 6:19 PM."

4. When you arrive, the app notes the real time. Each trip makes the next guess closer.

Questions

It didn't notice that I arrived.

Tap I'm Here on the trip screen. Automatic arrival needs location set to "While Using the App" with Precise Location on, in Settings > Apps > Honest ETA > Location.

One trip shouldn't count.

In History, swipe the trip and tap Don't Count. A stop for gas should not change your usual.

The time seems off.

At first the app goes by the guess you gave it. After a few trips it uses your real record, and it keeps adjusting.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a trip in History and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes everything.

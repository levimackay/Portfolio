Honest ETA privacy

Updated November 24, 2026

Honest ETA has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your trips stay on your phone.

Location

While you plan a trip, the app reads your location to get a travel time. While a trip is in progress it keeps reading it, including in the background, to notice when you arrive. It stops when the trip ends, is cancelled or times out. The app asks for "While Using the App" access and never asks for "Always".

Apple Maps

Place searches and travel times come from Apple Maps. Your search text and the start and end points of a trip are sent to Apple for that. Apple's privacy policy covers what Apple does with them.

Your trips

Where you went, the time you promised and the time you arrived are saved on your phone so the app can learn how late you run. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. If your phone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, the app's data is part of that backup like any other app's.

The message

The app writes the text. You choose who gets it and how, in the iOS share sheet. The app never sees your contacts or your messages.

Notifications

When the app notices you arrived, it may post a quiet notification on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone for this.

Age range

Where the law requires it, the app asks Apple for your age range when it opens. Nothing in the app depends on the answer and the app does not keep it.

What I receive

Nothing from the app. If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may show me anonymous totals such as downloads and crashes.

Deleting your data

Swipe a trip in History and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

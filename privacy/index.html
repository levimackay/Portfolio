Privacy

This site collects nothing about you.

It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. There are no forms. The fonts and scripts load from this site, so your browser talks to no one but the host.

The code editor on the home page and the izvor page runs in your browser. What you type there stays on your computer.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, both can log the IP address of a visitor, and Cloudflare shows me traffic totals such as how many requests the site served. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and the project sites take you to other sites with their own policies. If you email me, I use your address to reply and for nothing else.

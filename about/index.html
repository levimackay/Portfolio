About

The longer version.

Who is actually behind the four projects and the business on the work page.

I'm a CS student at BYU-Idaho, where I also TA programming courses and help other students write code that actually works. Outside of class I build in the Sandbox startup incubator, and I run Main Street Sites, a web design studio for small businesses in eastern Idaho: I built the product, set the pricing, and pitch it cold to real owners.

Before any of that I spent two years in the Balkans: Bosnia, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro, and Slovenia. Learning new languages and cultures meant constantly breaking unfamiliar problems into pieces I could actually work with, which is the same skill I use daily now, reading code I didn't write and figuring out what it's actually doing.

When I'm not at a keyboard I'm lifting, running, snowboarding, or on a dirt bike.

Teaching & mentoring
Code reviews, debugging sessions, and office hours as a TA for programming courses, catching the same handful of mistakes early before they turn into bad habits.
Sandbox incubator
I build in BYU-Idaho's Sandbox startup incubator, prototyping ideas outside of coursework and testing them against real feedback.
Balkan roots
Two years across five countries. I speak Bosnian, Croatian, and Serbian. Ask me about it.

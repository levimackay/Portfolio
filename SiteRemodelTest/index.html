About

Curious by default.
Disciplined by choice.

I'm a computer science student at BYU–Idaho, where I also work as a teaching assistant helping other students learn to write better code. Outside of class, I build products in the Sandbox startup incubator — mostly tools that use AI and automation to remove tedious work.

Before any of that, I spent two years serving across the Balkans — Bosnia, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro, and Slovenia. Learning to navigate new languages and cultures taught me how to break down unfamiliar problems, which turns out to be most of software engineering.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm lifting, running, snowboarding, or riding a dirt bike. Same principle everywhere: show up consistently and the results compound.

About.

Curious by default. Disciplined by choice.

I'm a CS student at BYU–Idaho, where I also TA programming courses and help other students write code that actually works. Outside of class I build in the Sandbox startup incubator, mostly tools that use AI and automation to get rid of tedious work.

Before any of that I spent two years in the Balkans: Bosnia, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro, and Slovenia. Figuring out new languages and cultures taught me how to break down problems I didn't understand yet. Turns out that's most of software engineering.

When I'm not at a keyboard I'm lifting, running, snowboarding, or on a dirt bike. Same rule everywhere: show up consistently and the results compound.
